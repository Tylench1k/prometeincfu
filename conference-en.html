Paper formatting requirements

  • Length

    Up to 6 pages.

  • Technical requirements

    Times New Roman, 1.5 line spacing, 14 pt (12 pt for the reference list). First-line indentation – 1.25 cm. Justified alignment. Margins: top and bottom – 2 cm; left – 3 cm; right – 1.5 cm.

  • Authors and title

    Author’s initials and surname on the right, in bold; below – the academic supervisor’s initials and surname; then the title of the paper in uppercase letters, also in bold.

  • Text and illustrations

    No word hyphenation. No automatic footnotes. Black-and-white graphs, charts and tables with captions may be included. Figures and illustrations are not permitted.

  • Typing rules

    No more than one space between words, no letter-spacing inside words, no space before punctuation marks. Distinguish between en dash and hyphen.

  • In-text references

    In square brackets: reference number and page – [1, p. 35]. Electronic resources without a page – [7]. A source cited earlier keeps its number. Several entries in ascending order, separated by semicolons: [10, p. 5; 20, p. 3; 25, p. 1].

  • Reference list

    In alphabetical order after the main text. One entry per number. Monographs and scholarly articles as print publications, without electronic resource information. References in Russian first, then in all other languages.
